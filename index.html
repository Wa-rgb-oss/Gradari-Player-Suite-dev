THE THREE SCALES OF PLAY

A place in the Empire

Personal choices. Faction ambitions. Imperial consequences.

01 ECONOMY & TRADE

Build your holdings

Extract resources, construct facilities, and trade through the Guilded Concord Exchange. Production, upkeep, and market prices shape the value of what you own.

02 FACTIONS & POLITICS

Extend your influence

Join other players in a faction, support its ambitions, and pursue a voice in the Empire. The Senate and Imperial offices connect personal influence with public power.

03 CHARACTERS & ROLEPLAY

Write your record

Create a character with their own motives and loyalties. Explore the map, collect artifacts, and submit actions for the Game Master to resolve within the shared world.

BEGIN YOUR RECORD

Your character. Your ambitions.

Create an account and character, explore the Player Suite, and read How to Play. Choose the interests and alliances that will guide your first actions.
