GRADARI MIRERIS: PLAYER REFERENCE THE GAME Gradari Mireris is a persistent strategy, roleplaying, and 4X game. Strategic play concerns factions, governments, economies, territories, fleets, armies, colonies, diplomacy, infrastructure, and research. Roleplaying concerns the individuals living inside those systems. Narrative play connects the two. CORE GAMEPLAY CYCLE OBSERVE → PLAN → ACT → RESOLVE → RECORD → ADAPT Players examine the situation, decide what they want to accomplish, act through roleplay or formal Actions, receive Game Master resolution, record meaningful consequences, and adapt to the resulting world. There is no permanent reset. PLAYER AGENCY Players are not limited to a fixed menu of actions. If something is reasonably possible within canon, it may be attempted. Greater ambitions normally require greater preparation, resources, authority, cooperation, or risk. CHARACTERS A player should normally maintain one Main Character and up to two active Side Characters. Each character remains limited by their own location, authority, knowledge, and responsibilities. WRITING AN ACTION A strong Action contains: Objective: What are you trying to accomplish? Method: How are you attempting it? Resources: What personnel, money, equipment, territory, authority, intelligence, or other assets are committed? Intent: What result do you actually want? THE RECORD Meaningful outcomes persist. Territory can change hands. Resources can be spent. Characters can gain authority or die. Treaties can become binding. Discoveries can permanently alter what factions know. GAME MASTER The Game Master resolves Actions, represents uncontrolled factions and characters, maintains hidden information and canon, and determines how the universe responds. The player determines the Action. The Game Master determines the response. MAJOR FACTIONS & POWERS Gradari Mireris Empire The dominant Imperial power and center of the known political order. Its government balances Martial, Civil, and Doctrinal authority through the Praetora, Pilea, and Ethrenari. Its vast territory contains Houses, institutions, military commands, corporations, and regional powers with interests of their own. Luminark Core A vast machine civilization in the eastern reaches of known space. It is governed by the Tri-Singularity of ARCH-LUX, VIRE-LUX, and TERA-LUX and pursues continuity through replication, reconstruction, archives, artificial populations, and controlled preservation. Accordant Spiral / Spiral Bloc A rebellious power and political movement resisting the stability and authority of the modern Empire. Rebellion within the Spiral Bloc is one of the major pressures upon the Era of Measured Equilibrium. Virelac Enclave An old and distinct non-Imperial civilization with its own interpretations of reality and the Immutable Laws. Its traditions include teachings radically different from those of the Ethrenari. Caelestis Concordat A solar-theocratic empire that treats starlight as divine law. Its Solar Mandate binds religion, government, and warfare together, regarding secrecy, doctrinal decay, and machine-born intellect as forms of heresy. Horizion Systems Compact A designed sovereignty built around continuity, competence, redundancy, and systemic stability. It maintains specialized systems, external trade, and security structures while preserving its independence. The Gilded Concord An established mercantile power organized around contracts, commerce, and the Ledger of Vows. Its influence is rooted in trade and negotiated obligation. The Aureum Order An unofficial political and financial bloc connected to elements of the Reserve Authority, merchant guilds, regional treasuries, House banks, and wealthy patrons. It uses debt, financial protection, and concealed influence to pursue its interests.
