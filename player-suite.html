CANON / PLAYER QUICK REFERENCE

Gradari Mireris

Gradari Mireris is a persistent strategy, roleplaying, and 4X project. Strategic play concerns factions, governments, economies, territories, fleets, armies, colonies, diplomacy, infrastructure, and research. Roleplaying concerns the people living inside those systems. Narrative play connects the two.

Core Gameplay Cycle

Observe → Plan → Act → Resolve → Record → Adapt. Players examine the situation, decide what they want to accomplish, act through roleplay or formal Actions, receive Game Master resolution, record meaningful consequences, and adapt to the resulting world.

Player Agency

Players are not limited to a fixed menu of actions. If something is reasonably possible within canon, it may be attempted. Greater ambitions normally require greater preparation, resources, authority, cooperation, or risk.

Characters

A player should normally maintain one Main Character and up to two active Side Characters. Each character remains limited by their own location, authority, knowledge, and responsibilities.

Writing an Action

A strong Action defines the Objective, Method, Resources, and Intent. These give the Game Master enough information to determine what happens and what consequences enter the Record.

The Record

Meaningful outcomes persist. Territory can change hands. Resources can be spent. Characters can gain authority or die. Treaties can become binding. Discoveries can permanently alter what factions know.

Game Master

The Game Master resolves Actions, represents uncontrolled factions and characters, maintains hidden information and canon, and determines how the universe responds. The player determines the Action. The Game Master determines the response.

Major Powers

Gradari Mireris Empire: the dominant Imperial power and center of the known political order. Its government balances Martial, Civil, and Doctrinal authority through the Praetora, Pilea, and Ethrenari.

Luminark Core: a vast machine civilization governed by the Tri-Singularity and focused on continuity, reconstruction, archives, artificial populations, and controlled preservation.

Accordant Spiral: a major non-Imperial power whose internal factions and relationships with the Empire shape a significant portion of regional politics.

Virelac Enclave: an old and distinct civilization with its own interpretations of reality and the Immutable Laws.

Caelestis Concordat: a solar-theocratic empire whose Solar Mandate binds religion, government, and warfare together.
